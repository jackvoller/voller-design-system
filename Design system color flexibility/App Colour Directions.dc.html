Turn 1 · in-app colour
Same system, different colour per app.
Type, geometry, spacing, structure, copy and motion are identical in both directions — only the palette is delegated to the app. Two options for how far the colour goes.
1a Accent duo, calm field
The page stays the house off-white. Each app owns a primary and one bright partner, spent on controls, checks, tags and the active tab. Vibrancy arrives as saturation, not as area.

What stays fixed in both
One platform-sans type ladder (34 title, 19/17 row heading, 15/14 body, 12 meta, 11 mono eyebrow); card 20–22, row 16, control 10–12, pill 999; 8px spacing with 12px gaps between cards; hierarchy from surface steps and hairlines, never a shadow; sentence-case British copy that names the verb and the absence ("on device", "44 of 44 in the trolley"); Lucide at 1.75px; 120/180ms colour and movement. Green and gold keep the icons, the website and the App Store — the apps inherit the rules, not the hues.
