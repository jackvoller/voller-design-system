Method

Heat the vegetable oil in a medium, deep frying pan over a medium heat and add the Thai green curry paste. Cook for 1 min until fragrant, then add the yellow pepper and green beans and briefly fry.
02
Add the coconut milk and bring to the boil. Reduce the heat, cover, and simmer for 8 mins, then add the prawns, cover and cook until they are just pink and curled, about 3 mins.

Section headings were 22px grey titles competing with the page title; they are now mono eyebrows, so "Settings" is the only heading on screen. Footnotes sit at 13px under their group. Lois's purple avatar takes the house cream, and only the remove control carries the alert token.
The four rules applied
One header shape
Title with the green full stop, a 13px status line under it, and the floating icon cluster unpacked into 34px glass circles on the same baseline — so the eye lands in the same place on all four tabs.
Mono eyebrows, not grey headings
"Green grocery", "Account", "Week setup" were 22px semibold greys — a second title on every screen. At 11px mono uppercase they label without competing.
One white card, 22px, 12px margin
Rows are separated by the house hairline rather than by gaps between floating capsules, which is what made Shop feel like a different app from Plan.
Green once per screen
The trolley bar and its ticks; the chosen filter; the switch that is on. Everything else is ink on white with a #F3F1EC neutral for secondary controls — and the black pill button is gone.
Text sizes match Plan
32 title, 26 hero, 16 row, 13 secondary, 11 mono. The screenshots ran rows at ~19px, which is why three items filled a screen.
Turn 2 · tap a day, edit the meal
The day opens a glass sheet. The plan stays behind it.
Built on 1b, and working: tap tonight's card or any day below it to open the sheet, tap the meal or Swap to reach the picker, choose something and it lands back in the plan. A day with nothing planned skips the sheet and opens the picker directly. The sheet is liquid glass — translucent material over the plan, specular top edge, floating capsule controls — with the sheet's own colour rules applied: green is Done, remove is the alert token, nothing is purple.

What changed in the sheet
The title bar does the job of the title card
"Tuesday Dinner" was said twice — once in the nav bar, once in a full-width card. The card is gone and the date became the mono line under the title, which returns about 120pt to the content.
Glass, but layered properly
One material for the sheet, a lighter one for the cards, a brighter one for the capsule controls, and specular top edges on all three — so depth comes from the material rather than from shadows.
Swap and remove lost their own hues
Purple and pink tinted circles became glass capsules: Swap is labelled rather than guessed at, and remove keeps the house alert token as its glyph colour only.
"Cooking for" moved up
It is the answer the toggles produce, so it sits on the section header and updates as you switch people. Guests gained a stepper instead of a bare on/off.
One green per state
Done in edit, the filter chip in the picker, and the tick on the chosen meal. Toggles are the one exception the platform earns.
Turn 1 · Meal Planner plan screen
Three ways to read a week.
The shipping screen shows one and a half days per viewport: every day is a card, every day repeats the Home/Away switch, and every meal repeats a pair of full-width buttons. All three redesigns keep the six Meal Planner tokens and spend the accent once per view — they differ in how much of the week you can see at once.
Fixed everywhere
Title and its controls share one line. Home/Away leaves the day header — it appears only when a day is away, or inside the day you are looking at.
One accent per view
Green is the cook action. Today is marked by a mono pill and weight, not by a green band; a filled green header with a green button under it was two golds.
People stay neutral
Avatars are warm greys at 22px. Nothing in the plan is purple, and no colour is used to identify a person.

What each one costs you
1a is the cheapest to build and the best for editing a week, but tonight is no more prominent than Friday. 1b answers "what am I cooking?" instantly and still shows the week, at the cost of one screen doing two jobs. 1c is the only one with room for photos and more than one meal a day — say the word if lunches and breakfasts are coming, because that is the one that survives them.
