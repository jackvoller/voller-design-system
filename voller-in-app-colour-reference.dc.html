Turn 2 · one accent per app
Six tokens per app. One accent per screen.
1b's tinted field, with the second and third hues taken back out. UnJumble is coral, Meal Planner is a vivid green, and everything else in the app is ink, muted and white. Tags, meta and counts are neutral now — colour only marks the action, the current thing, and the danger.
2a The token model
Every app publishes exactly these six, and inherits everything else. An app may not add a seventh colour to its chrome; if it needs a distinction, it moves inside its own accent ramp.

Inherited, never overridden
Ink #1B1B19, cream #F6F1E3, muted #6E6E6B / #A09892, surfaces white / #2B2220-family, hairlines, and the alert pair #C4382C / #E8705F for destruction only.
The label follows the fill
Light accents carry an ink label — ink on coral is 5.6:1, on green 7.1:1, where cream would be 2.6:1. Dark accents flip: cream on Riverly's #0B5FD0 is 5.2:1. Each app publishes which of the two its fill takes, and never guesses per screen.
Accent once per role
The gold rule, generalised: one primary action, one active tab, one "current" marker. Counts, tags, dates and durations are neutral — that is what keeps it minimal at speed.
The accent draws the app's icon too
Updated 1 Sep 2026. voller.uk, the App Store and the Voller mark are yellow #FFC400; the four app icons are drawn in their own accents, not in a house colour. Meal Planner's and UnPickle's accent is the old brand green, so the family resemblance survives where the shop is. See VOLLER.md §1 and §4.1.

If you're happy with 2b–2f, here's what changes in the system
§4 colour splits into house (green, gold, cream — icons, voller.uk, App Store) and app (the six tokens, published per app). The gold rule becomes the accent rule: one accent per role per view. "No new hues in chrome" survives as "no hue outside your own six". The alert pair, the type ladder, radii, spacing, motion, hairline hierarchy, Lucide at 1.75px and the whole voice section stay exactly as they are — and I'd add one new rule: neutral by default, so counts, dates, tags and avatars never take the accent.
